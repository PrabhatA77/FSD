WELCOME TO ABES ENGINEERING COLLEGE

DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE

The Department came into inception from year 2018-19 with an intake of 60 seats as B.Tech CEIT (Computer Engineering & Information Technology) branch. Later on in the year 2019-20, two more AICTE approved streams such as Computer Engineering (60 seats) and Computer Science (60 seats) were introduced in the department, in addition to CEIT. Now, from 2020-21 onwards the Department of Computer Science is running with an intake of 180 seats.It has become the most sought after department among engineering aspirants. The department has a team of experienced, highly qualified and motivated faculty members. The Department believes in nurturing the students with the ability and skills to analyze, design and develop computer systems and their applications, with a curriculum that has a balanced emphasis on theory, practical and projects.
